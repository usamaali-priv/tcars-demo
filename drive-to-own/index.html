Frequently Asked Questions

Good to know before you apply.

Ready to drive? Here's what you need

Applying for Drive-to-Own is simple. Whether you are employed or self-employed, you can apply as long as you have the following documents:

1. Personal identification

  • National ID
  • Valid driver's licence
  • 2 passport-size photographs

2. Proof of residence

Any ONE of the following:

  • Recent ZESA bill
  • Recent council bill (not older than 3 months)
  • Affidavit confirming your residential address

3. Proof of income

Any ONE of the following:

  • Last 3 months' payslips
  • Last 3 months' bank statements
  • Last 3 months' EcoCash statements

4. Employment or business details

If employed:

  • Employer confirmation letter

If self-employed or running a business:

  • 2 referees
  • Business cashbook showing your income and transactions

You can apply!

You don't necessarily need to be formally employed. Whether you work for a company or run your own business, you can apply provided you can supply the relevant supporting documents.

How long can I repay over?

6, 12, 18, 24, 30 or 36 months on both packages. After the last payment the car is yours.

Can I use Drive-to-Own for used cars?

Yes. Pre-owned cars from 2016 and newer go on Package B, with a 31% minimum deposit. A $9,000 car is $2,790 to start, then $369.46 a month over 36 months.

What if the car costs more than $15,000?

Drive-to-own covers up to $15,000 of the price. You pay the rest as a top-up together with your deposit: a $20,000 car is a $5,000 top-up plus the deposit, and the monthly payments are worked out on the $15,000.

Which cars qualify?

Cars from 2016 and newer, priced from $3,000. Brand new cars go on Package A (26% deposit), pre-owned on Package B (31% deposit).

What if the car I want isn't in stock?

You can still apply. Use Apply now and choose "A car I've seen elsewhere" or "Help me find one". We can source most cars in 3 to 5 days.

Can I pay earlier?

Yes. Faster repayment may reduce applicable fees depending on the selected plan.

Are these calculations final?

No. They are estimates only. The TC team confirms final prices when you apply.
